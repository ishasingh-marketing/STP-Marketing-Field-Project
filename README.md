# STP Marketing Immersion Project – Uniqlo, Starbucks & Samsung

## Project Overview

This project explores the application of the Segmentation, Targeting, and Positioning (STP) framework through a field-based marketing study at Ambience Mall, Delhi.

As part of the Marketing in Practice assignment at the Fortune Institute of International Business (FIIB), the project examines how three prominent brands—Uniqlo, Starbucks, and Samsung—identify their target customers, cater to different consumer needs, and establish distinctive positions in competitive markets.

The study connects classroom marketing concepts with real-world retail observations to understand how brands attract customers, differentiate their offerings, and communicate value.

## Project Objectives

- Understand the practical application of the STP framework.
- Analyse customer segmentation strategies across different industries.
- Identify the target audiences of Uniqlo, Starbucks, and Samsung.
- Examine how each brand differentiates itself from competitors.
- Observe retail environments and brand communication at a shopping mall.
- Connect theoretical marketing concepts with real-world brand strategies.

## Brands Analysed

### 1. Uniqlo – Apparel and Fashion Retail
The analysis explores how Uniqlo caters to consumers seeking functional, comfortable, and versatile clothing. It examines potential customer segments based on lifestyle, preferences, and clothing needs, along with the brand's positioning around everyday essentials and functional innovation.

### 2. Starbucks – Coffee and Café Experience
The analysis examines Starbucks' approach to customer segmentation, including lifestyle, consumption occasions, and café experience preferences. It considers how the brand differentiates itself through its coffee offerings, store atmosphere, personalisation, and experience-led positioning.

### 3. Samsung – Consumer Electronics
The analysis explores Samsung's broad consumer base across smartphones and other electronic products. It considers segmentation based on customer needs, technology preferences, lifestyle, and purchasing power, alongside the brand's positioning around technological innovation and product variety.

## STP Framework

### Segmentation
Dividing the market into meaningful customer groups based on relevant characteristics such as demographics, lifestyle, preferences, purchasing behaviour, and product needs.

### Targeting
Identifying the customer segments a brand seeks to serve and understanding how its offerings align with their expectations.

### Positioning
Examining how a brand creates a distinctive identity in consumers' minds through its value proposition, product features, customer experience, and communication.

## Research Approach

The project uses a field-based learning approach involving observations at Ambience Mall, Delhi, and an analysis of the selected brands through the STP framework.

The study aims to connect brand positioning and target-market decisions with observable retail experiences and customer-facing brand elements.

- Understanding of customer needs, preferences, and target-market selection.
